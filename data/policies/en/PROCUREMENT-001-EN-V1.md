# Procurement Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This internal policy of Atlas Services, a fictional Moroccan company, applies to purchases made on behalf of the company and paid by it through a purchase order. Employee-paid expenses follow the Professional Expenses Policy.

## 2. Requests and budget authorization

The requester describes the need, estimated cost, cost center, and desired date. The budget owner verifies the need and budget availability and records their approval. The requester provides the quote, supplier terms, and reasons for selecting the supplier for review before any commitment.

**Conditional approval.** If the budget is authorized and the supplier documentation is complete, Finance may approve the purchase order before any commitment to the supplier.

The ordering team sends the approved purchase order to the supplier. Any change to price or scope goes back to the budget owner and Finance for approval before confirmation.

## 3. Separation of responsibilities

| Step | Responsible party | What to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Request | Requester | Need, estimate, and cost center |
| Budget authorization | Budget owner | Need and available budget |
| Purchase order approval | Finance | Budget approval and supplier documentation |
| Ordering and receipt | Ordering team and person responsible for receipt | Approved order and delivery conformity |

The requester does not approve their own request. The person who sends the order is different from the person who approves it. Receipt is recorded separately to reconcile the purchase order, delivery, and invoice.

## 4. Purchases involving an order and an employee advance

For a transaction involving a purchase order and an employee-paid expense, identify each line item and its payment route. Company-paid lines follow the purchase-order approvals. For employee-paid lines, obtain the applicable prior approvals under the Professional Expenses Policy before committing the expense, then submit the claim with supporting documents. The purchase order does not waive those approvals. A line item may be paid through only one route.

## 5. Exceptions and escalation

**Exception.** If a need is urgent, the budget owner and Finance may prioritize review before commitment, with a written explanation of the urgency.

The approved purchase order and required approvals still apply. If there is a dispute about the budget, supplier, or payment route, the requester refers the case to Finance and waits for a decision before making any commitment.
