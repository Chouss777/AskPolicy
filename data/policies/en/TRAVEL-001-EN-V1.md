# Business Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This internal policy of Atlas Services, a fictional Moroccan company, applies to employee travel on company assignments in Morocco and abroad. It sets the rules for approval, booking, and reimbursement. The limits below are fictional company limits.

## 2. Trip authorization

1. The employee submits a request to their line manager at least five working days before the planned departure. The request includes the business purpose, dates, destination, cost center, and estimated budget.
2. The line manager verifies the need for the trip and the availability of funds, then records the decision in the request.
3. The employee sends the approved request to Operations for booking.

**Conditional approval.** If the total estimated cost exceeds 3,000 MAD, both the line manager and Finance must approve the request before any booking is made.

## 3. Booking responsibilities

Operations books transport and accommodation based on the approved request. The employee confirms the itinerary and reports any changes as soon as they become aware of them. Operations checks any change fees. A change that takes the estimated cost above the approval threshold must follow the procedure in section 2 before it is confirmed.

## 4. Transport and accommodation

| Expense | Internal rule | Supporting document |
|---|---|---|
| Train | Second class when the train journey allows the employee to carry out the assignment at the required times | Ticket in the employee's name |
| Air travel | Economy class if the train journey cannot accommodate the assignment's schedule | Ticket and invoice |
| Accommodation | Limit of 850 MAD per night | Itemized invoice |
| Local transport | Taxi or public transport for trips related to the assignment | Receipt or ticket |

## 5. Expense reimbursement

The employee submits an expense claim within ten working days of returning. The claim includes supporting documents, the approved request reference, and the reason for the expenses. Finance reimburses documented business expenses within the applicable limits. Personal expenses and costs paid directly by Atlas Services are excluded from the claim. If a supporting document is missing, Finance puts the line item on hold and asks for replacement documentation.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation

**Exception.** In a safety emergency, the traveler may book necessary transport without prior approval.

The traveler notifies Operations and their line manager as soon as possible, then sends Finance the supporting document and the reason for the emergency within two working days. Outside an emergency, the traveler submits any request to exceed the accommodation limit to Finance before booking, with the alternatives considered. If the line manager and Finance disagree, Operations leadership resolves the matter before any expense is committed.
