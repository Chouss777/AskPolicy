# Professional Expenses Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This internal policy of Atlas Services, a fictional Moroccan company, sets out how to claim reimbursement for professional expenses. It covers expenses an employee pays out of pocket to carry out an approved assignment for the company. The limits are fictional company rules.

## 2. Eligible expenses

| Expense | Eligibility condition or limit | Supporting document |
|---|---|---|
| Meal during a field assignment | Limit of 150 MAD per meal if the company does not provide the meal as part of the assignment | Itemized receipt |
| Operational supplies | Supplies necessary for the assignment and charged to the relevant cost center | Itemized invoice and proof of payment |
| Transport and accommodation during business travel | Conditions and limits in the Business Travel Policy | Documents required by the Business Travel Policy |

Personal expenses, amounts paid directly by the company, and expenses already reimbursed are excluded.

## 3. Claim submission and supporting documents

The employee submits the claim through the internal expenses channel within ten working days of the date of the expense. Business travel claims follow the submission deadline in the Business Travel Policy.

1. The employee records the date, amount, business purpose, and cost center for each expense.
2. The employee attaches the documents listed in the table and the prior approval reference when required.
3. The employee sends the claim to the line manager for review and provides any additional documents Finance requests.

## 4. Review and approval

The line manager verifies the link to the assignment and the correct cost center. Finance checks the documentation, the applicable limit, and whether the expense has already been reimbursed.

**Conditional approval.** If the estimated cost of operational purchases exceeds 1,000 MAD, both the line manager and Finance must approve the purchase before the expense is committed.

## 5. Reimbursement conditions

After review, Finance reimburses the amount actually paid for eligible expenses up to the applicable limit. If a document is missing or a record needs correction, Finance holds the affected line item and tells the employee what is needed. Review continues for complete line items.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation

**Exception.** If the original receipt cannot be obtained, Finance may accept a supplier-issued copy with proof of payment and an explanation of why the original is unavailable.

If the alternative documentation cannot be completed, the line item remains on hold. The employee may request a review by the Finance lead, who reviews the available documents and records the decision in the claim.
