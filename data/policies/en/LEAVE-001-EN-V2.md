# Leave and Absence Policy

## 1. Purpose and HR scope

This internal procedure of Atlas Services, a fictional Moroccan company, describes how Human Resources (HR) processes leave and absence requests. HR checks balances, reviews cases, and records decisions. The line manager assesses service continuity. The deadlines and thresholds below are fictional company rules.

## 2. Request submission and processing

1. For planned leave, the employee submits the requested dates at least five working days before departure. The employee sends any requested confidential supporting document directly to HR.
2. HR checks the dates and the available balance. If the request is incomplete, HR tells the employee what information to provide.
3. The line manager assesses the impact on the service. HR records the decision and notifies the employee. The request remains pending until HR sends that notification.

## 3. Leave validation

**Conditional approval.** If a request for ordinary leave covers more than five consecutive working days, the line manager confirms service continuity and HR validates the balance before notifying the employee of approval.

If the available balance does not cover the requested period, HR asks for revised dates before deciding. If there is a scheduling conflict, the line manager gives HR the service constraints and alternative dates. HR communicates the decision and rescheduling options to the employee.

## 4. Confidentiality and information sharing

The processing register, individual balances, and supporting documents are reserved for HR. The line manager receives only the dates and status needed to organize service coverage. If the line manager receives a supporting document by mistake, they forward it to HR through the confidential internal channel without copying the team. The shared schedule contains no detailed medical or family reasons.

| Recipient | Information shared | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| HR team | Case file, balance, decision, and necessary supporting documents | Review the request |
| Line manager | Requested dates and request status | Maintain service continuity |
| Requesting employee | Decision on their request and steps that concern them | Follow up on their request |

## 5. Exceptions and escalation

**Exception.** In a family emergency, HR may authorize an absence without prior validation by the line manager.

HR tells the line manager the dates needed for service coverage. The employee completes the formal request within two working days after returning. To dispute a balance or decision, the employee refers the case to HR leadership, which reviews the case again and records its decision in the HR-only register.
