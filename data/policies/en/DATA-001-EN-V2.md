# Data Handling Policy

## 1. Purpose and responsibilities

This internal policy of Atlas Services, a fictional Moroccan company, applies to creating, storing, and sharing work data. Before sharing, employees verify the classification, the business need, and the recipient's authorization.

## 2. Classification and sharing

| Classification | What to verify | Handling rule |
|---|---|---|
| PUBLIC | Authorization of the recipient and the planned distribution | Use the approved distribution channel |
| INTERNAL | Business need and recipient authorization | Share only the necessary items |
| RESTRICTED | Explicit authorization of the recipient for the document | Use the authorized space and share only what is strictly necessary |

Classification alone confers no access. If recipient authorization is unknown, the employee suspends sharing and seeks confirmation from the data owner. Confidential files remain subject to their own restrictions.

## 3. Storage and transfer

Employees use company-approved storage spaces and transfer channels for the data concerned. Before sending, they check the recipient, the list of attachments, and the need for each item. Working copies containing confidential data stay in authorized spaces.

**Conditional approval.** If transfer to an external recipient is necessary, the data owner and IT Security must approve the transfer before it is sent.

Employees use the approved transfer channel and follow the applicable access restrictions.

## 4. Accidental disclosure

Employees promptly report any suspected disclosure to IT Security under the Incident Reporting Policy. They specify the channel, time, and category of the affected data without redistributing it. They follow the instructions they receive to limit disclosure.

## 5. Exceptions and escalation

**Exception.** If the usual channel is unavailable, the data owner and IT Security may approve temporary use of another authorized channel before any transfer.

The decision records the justification, duration, and selected channel. Recipient restrictions continue to apply. If no authorized channel is available, the employee suspends the transfer and contacts IT Security.
