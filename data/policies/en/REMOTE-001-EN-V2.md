# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This internal policy of Atlas Services, a fictional Moroccan company, governs remote work for activities the line manager considers suitable for remote delivery. Service organization, employee availability, and data protection requirements continue to apply during remote work.

## 2. Requests and authorization

The employee proposes the days, the period covered, and their availability arrangements to the line manager. The line manager checks that the tasks can be performed remotely and that service coverage can be maintained.

**Conditional approval.** If the tasks and service coverage allow it, the line manager may authorize up to two remote-work days per week in advance.

The employee waits for approval before working remotely. The line manager sends the approved days and period to HR.

## 3. Tracking approved arrangements

| Party | Responsibility | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| Employee | Propose days and report changes to the line manager | Requested days and availability arrangements |
| Line manager | Verify service coverage and authorize the arrangements | Approved days and period |
| HR | Record approved arrangements and changes | Current arrangements and change date |

Any change to an approved arrangement follows the same procedure before taking effect. HR keeps the record up to date.

## 4. Remote work and data protection

Atlas Services' data handling rules continue to apply remotely. Employees use approved work tools and approved storage and transfer channels, limit sharing to authorized recipients, and report incidents under the Incident Reporting Policy.

## 5. Exceptions and interruptions

**Exception.** If a temporary need justifies exceeding two remote-work days per week, the line manager must approve the request and HR must document its review before the arrangement is implemented.

The line manager sends HR the justification, the requested duration, and the planned continuity measures. If an interruption prevents remote work, the employee informs the line manager and they agree on an alternative that meets service needs.
