# Corpus Rule Matrix

This working registry tracks Batch 1 consistency for the fictional Atlas Services corpus. The corpus, versioning, and authorization contracts remain in `docs/decisions.md` §§3–4 and §6.

## A. Established rules in the four source policies

| Rule | Established requirement | Source document and section |
|---|---|---|
| Travel planned request | At least five working days before planned departure, the employee sends the line manager the business purpose, dates, destination, cost center, and estimated budget. The manager checks necessity and budget and records the decision. The employee sends the approved request to Operations. | TRAVEL-001-FR-V1 §2 « Autorisation de la mission » and TRAVEL-001-EN-V1 §2 “Trip authorization” |
| Travel dual approval | If the total estimated trip cost exceeds 3,000 MAD, both the line manager and Finance must approve before any booking. | TRAVEL-001-FR-V1 §2 « Autorisation de la mission » and TRAVEL-001-EN-V1 §2 “Trip authorization” |
| Travel booking and changes | Operations books from the approved request and checks change fees. The traveler confirms the itinerary and promptly reports changes. A change that takes the estimate above the approval threshold follows the dual-approval procedure before confirmation. | TRAVEL-001-FR-V1 §3 « Réservation et responsabilités » and TRAVEL-001-EN-V1 §3 “Booking responsibilities” |
| Travel transport | Use second-class rail when the schedule permits the assignment, and economy air travel when rail cannot meet the assignment schedule. Local taxi or public transport must relate to the assignment. Keep the named rail ticket, air ticket and invoice, or local receipt or ticket as applicable. | TRAVEL-001-FR-V1 §4 « Transport et hébergement » and TRAVEL-001-EN-V1 §4 “Transport and accommodation” |

| Travel claim | Submit within ten working days after return, with supporting documents, the approved request reference, and the expense purpose. Finance reimburses documented professional expenses within applicable limits, excludes personal and company-paid costs, and holds any line missing documentation while requesting a replacement. | TRAVEL-001-FR-V1 §5 « Remboursement des frais » and TRAVEL-001-EN-V1 §5 “Expense reimbursement” |

## B. Fictional rules introduced in Batch 1

| Document and sections | Rule introduced |
|---|---|
| `REMOTE-001-FR-V2` §§1–5 | Employees propose remote days and an availability plan. The normal limit is two remote-work days per week with prior manager approval. HR records approved arrangements and changes. Company data-handling rules continue to apply. Exceptions require prior documented HR review in addition to manager approval. Report an interruption to the manager and agree on an alternative arrangement. |
| `ACCESS-001-FR-V2` §§1–5 | Access requests require a documented business need, manager validation, and IT Security approval before access is granted. Limit grants to the necessary scope and duration. Review on role changes and at scheduled reviews, and revoke unnecessary access. | `DATA-001-FR-V2` §§1–5 | Check classification, recipient authorization, and purpose before sharing. Use approved storage and transfer channels and share the minimum necessary. Report accidental disclosure promptly to IT Security. External transfers and exceptional channel changes require prior approval from the data owner and IT Security, with existing access restrictions applied. |
| `INCIDENT-001-FR-V1` §§1–5 | Report suspected incidents promptly through the designated IT Security channel. Record observed facts and preserve evidence through approved channels. IT Security performs initial triage and must approve restoration that would alter evidence. If the designated channel is unavailable, alert the manager through an approved alternative for relay to IT Security. |
| `PROCUREMENT-001-FR-V1` §§1–5 | Company-issued purchases require a request, documented budget approval, supplier documentation, and Finance authorization of the purchase order before commitment. Separate requesting, approving, ordering, and receiving responsibilities as specified. Urgency may accelerate review while approvals and the approved purchase order remain required. Employee advances follow Expenses approval and claim rules. |
| `LEAVE-001-EN-V2`, `EXPENSES-001-FR-V2`, `EXPENSES-001-EN-V2` | Language counterparts carry forward the established source rules in section A. |

## F. Cross-policy distinctions and exceptions

- Expenses applies to reimbursement of employee-paid business costs. Travel transport, lodging, evidence, and claim timing follow Travel. The ten-working-day travel deadline starts at return. The ten-working-day ordinary expense deadline starts at the expense.
- Procurement applies to company-issued purchase orders. A purchase order does not satisfy required approval for an employee-funded expense. For a transaction involving both routes, record each route and obtain its applicable approvals before committing funds. Keep each cost on a single payment or reimbursement route.
- Remote Work and Data Handling apply authorized channels and least-necessary sharing. Data Handling and Incident Reporting give general handling and reporting duties. The restricted Leave and Access Control policies remain subject to their explicit ACLs.
- Travel's safety emergency exception applies to necessary transport. Leave's family emergency exception covers absence authorization. The Expenses exception concerns replacement receipt evidence. Each exception has its own conditions.
